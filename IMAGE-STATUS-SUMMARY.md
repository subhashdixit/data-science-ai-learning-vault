## 🎨 Image Standards

### Technical Specs:
- **Format:** PNG (lossless, good for diagrams)
- **Resolution:** 1200×800 pixels minimum
- **Color Scheme:** Match repository colors (💙💚❤️💛💜)
- **Fonts:** Minimum 14pt for readability
- **Background:** White or transparent

### Content Guidelines:
- **Clear labels** on all axes and elements
- **Legends** where needed for multi-series plots
- **Annotations** to highlight key concepts
- **Consistent style** across similar diagram types
- **Professional quality** suitable for learning
